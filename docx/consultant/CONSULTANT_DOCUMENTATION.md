## Timesheet Management

### Understanding Timesheets (`/consultant/timesheet/`)

Timesheets are the foundation of your billing and project tracking. Accurate timesheet management ensures proper compensation and project accountability.

#### Timesheet Structure

**Basic Timesheet Entry:**
```
- Date: When the work was performed
- Start Time: Work session beginning
- End Time: Work session completion
- Duration: Calculated work hours
- Project/Client: Associated project
- Task Description: What was accomplished
- Billable/Non-billable: Billing status
```

#### Creating Timesheet Entries

**Manual Entry Process:**
1. Navigate to Timesheet section
2. Click "Add New Entry"
3. Fill in entry details:
   - **Date Selection**: Choose work date
   - **Time Entry**: Start and end times
   - **Project Association**: Select client/project
   - **Task Description**: Detailed work description
   - **Billing Status**: Mark as billable or non-billable
4. Save the entry
5. Review for accuracy

**Bulk Entry Options:**
- Upload CSV timesheet files
- Copy previous entries
- Create recurring entries for regular tasks

#### Timesheet File Upload

**CSV Upload Process:**
1. Download the timesheet template
2. Fill in your time entries using Excel/Google Sheets
3. Save as CSV format
4. Upload via the timesheet interface
5. Review imported entries
6. Correct any errors
7. Submit for approval

**CSV Format Requirements:**
```csv
Date,Start Time,End Time,Project,Task Description,Billable
2024-01-15,09:00,12:00,Client A,Requirements analysis,Yes
2024-01-15,13:00,17:00,Client A,System design,Yes
2024-01-16,10:00,11:30,Admin,Team meeting,No
```

#### Timesheet Management Features

**Entry Editing:**
- Modify existing entries (if not yet submitted)
- Add forgotten time entries
- Correct errors in descriptions or times
- Update billing status

**Time Tracking Tools:**
- Built-in timer for real-time tracking
- Break time management
- Automatic duration calculation
- Time rounding options

**Reporting and Analytics:**
- Daily/weekly/monthly summaries
- Billable vs. non-billable hours
- Project time distribution
- Productivity trends

#### Timesheet Approval Process

**Submission Workflow:**
1. **Entry Creation**: Log your work hours
2. **Review Period**: Check entries for accuracy
3. **Submission**: Submit timesheet for approval
4. **Admin Review**: Administrator reviews entries
5. **Approval/Rejection**: Status notification received
6. **Invoice Generation**: Approved hours ready for billing

**Timesheet Status Types:**
- **Draft**: Work in progress, can be edited
- **Submitted**: Sent for review, limited editing
- **Approved**: Accepted by administrator
- **Rejected**: Requires corrections and resubmission
- **Invoiced**: Included in billing, locked

## Invoice Generation

### Invoice Management (`/consultant/invoices/`)

The invoice system streamlines your billing process and ensures timely payment for your consulting services.

#### Understanding Invoices

**Invoice Components:**
```
- Invoice Number: Unique identifier
- Billing Period: Date range covered
- Client Information: Who is being billed
- Service Details: Work performed
- Hours and Rates: Time and pricing breakdown
- Total Amount: Final billing amount
- Payment Terms: When payment is due
- Status: Current invoice state
```

#### Creating Invoices

**Invoice Creation Process:**
1. Navigate to Invoice section
2. Click "Create New Invoice"
3. Select billing period and approved timesheets
4. Review automatically calculated amounts
5. Add any additional charges or adjustments
6. Include relevant notes or descriptions
7. Generate invoice PDF
8. Submit for administrator approval

**Invoice Generation Options:**

**Option 1: Automatic Generation**
- System creates invoices from approved timesheets
- Calculates totals based on hourly rates
- Includes all billable hours for selected period
- Applies standard payment terms

**Option 2: Manual Creation**
- Custom invoice amounts and descriptions
- Project-based billing instead of hourly
- Special rates or discounts
- Additional expenses or materials

#### Invoice Details and Customization

**Standard Invoice Information:**
- Your professional details and contact information
- Client billing information
- Detailed breakdown of services provided
- Hours worked and applicable rates
- Subtotals, taxes (if applicable), and total amount
- Payment instructions and terms

**Customization Options:**
- Add your professional logo
- Include project-specific descriptions
- Attach supporting documentation
- Add payment terms and conditions
- Include tax information (if required)

#### Invoice Status Management

**Invoice Lifecycle:**
```
Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved → Sent → Paid
```

**Status Descriptions:**
- **Draft**: Invoice being prepared, can be edited
- **Submitted**: Sent to administrator for review
- **Under Review**: Administrator is reviewing details
- **Approved**: Administrator has approved invoice
- **Sent**: Invoice has been sent to client
- **Paid**: Payment has been received
- **Rejected**: Invoice needs corrections
- **Cancelled**: Invoice has been cancelled

#### Payment Tracking

**Payment Management:**
- Track invoice payment status
- Monitor overdue invoices
- Set up payment reminders
- Record partial payments
- Generate payment reports

**Payment Methods:**
- Direct bank transfer
- Check payments
- Online payment systems
- Platform-managed payments (if available)

#### Invoice Analytics

**Financial Reporting:**
- Monthly earnings summaries
- Year-to-date income tracking
- Average payment times
- Outstanding invoice amounts
- Client payment patterns
